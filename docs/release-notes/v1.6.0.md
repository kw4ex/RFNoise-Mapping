# RF Noise Map v1.6.0

The tidy-up release: a much shorter, laptop-friendly screen. Everything from 1.5.0 is included.

> **Status:** shared as is, no warranty, use at your own risk. Tested with simulated hardware and on a Windows 11 PC and a Mac with a real SDRplay RSP1, a real Hermes Lite 2, a real ANAN-7000DLE MkIII and a GPS. Not yet confirmed in a long real drive with every radio, and the RTL-SDR dongle support has only been tried with simulated dongles. Design and on-air testing by KW4EX.

## What is new

- **Each slot has its own S-meter**, drawn inside the slot at the lower right, with the big S reading and the dB and peak readings inside the meter face and a **Reset peak** button on every meter. The **S-meters** tick box is in Settings.
- **A one-line slot heading:** slot number and frequency, **Pop out**, a padlock (**Lock**), **Zoom**, the live dB and S reading, and the radio.
- **Sound controls in the slot:** a speaker icon and volume slider, **Mute** (reads **Listen** while muted), **Squelch** and **Record**. One Mute/Listen button replaces the old Listen, Mute and tick-box controls.
- **The More button is gone.** Its contents moved into the slot: a **Band** menu, **Width**, **Auto scale**, and small pop-up boxes for **Gain**, **Memory** and (SDRplay) **Band view**. A red **OVERLOAD** shows on the slot when the Hermes overloads.
- **ANAN slots can now be heard and recorded**, with the same modes, **AGC** and **Zoom** as the Hermes. Checked against a simulated ANAN; not yet heard on the real radio.
- **A shorter top bar:** the four slot boxes, the info lines above the slots and the row of buttons above the slots are gone. **Max speed test** and **Health check** moved into **Tools…**. The Hermes picture, **Compact view** and **Free the radios for other programs** moved into **Settings**.
- **Drives & maps…** and **Tools…** open as pop-up windows from the top bar (close with the button, Esc, or a click outside). The "Jump to" row is gone.
- **The top row shrinks to fit** if you make the window narrower, so **Car mode** no longer drops to a second line, also while recording.
- **Compact view is the default**, the page uses the full window width (up to 2000 px), and the layout was tested at 1280 and 1440 pixels wide.
- **A Restart button** (beside Close program) saves any drive being recorded, closes the program and starts it again with a fresh browser tab.
- **Clearer names:** **Check setup** is now **Health check**, and **Test my setup** is now **Max speed test**. The Strong signal box has a **Sound & voice** button.
- **Settings…** is one window with how often to take a reading, pause when stopped, your radios, the GPS, the gain settings and the display options.
