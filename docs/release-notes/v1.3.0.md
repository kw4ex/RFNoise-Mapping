# RF Noise Map v1.3.0

The multi-radio release: the Hermes Lite 2 joins the SDRplay, each frequency slot picks its radio, and maps can space their dots evenly by distance.

- **Switching fix for the SDRplay (from real-hardware tests).** A strong signal's leftover data was making a quiet channel read up to about 55 dB too high right after a jump. The program now waits longer (about 33 ms) only when it jumps from a much stronger frequency to a weaker one, and re-reads once if a reading suddenly drops far below that channel's last level. Switch_Test.bat gained a table of wait sizes to confirm this.
- **Hermes Lite 2 support (network cable, HF only).** Several receivers run at the same time, so every frequency is read at the same instant with no retuning. No driver is needed (plain network packets). New: radio picker, Find my radio, radio gain, detail per frequency, HL2_Test.bat (found? streaming cleanly? mirrored? gain? overload?), HL2 awareness in Check setup and the full test. Tested against a simulated radio; not yet on a real one.
- **Slots 1-4.** Each frequency is a slot: a live panadapter with a waterfall, its own tuning, mode, width, zoom, hand-set or automatic dB scale, gain slider, volume/mute (any number of slots can play) and recording. Slots start by themselves, remember their settings, and can be popped out into their own full-size browser tabs or shown all four on one screen (compact view). While logging, Hermes slots stay fully live; an SDRplay slot shows its latest reading and history until you pause. Pause recording (or Pause automatically after a number of seconds standing still) brings every slot back to a full live picture.
- **Every reading is timed to the millisecond.** The CSV now has the time of each reading and the time its position refers to (new column pos_time_utc). Old files still work.
- **Maps with one dot every X feet.** Choose the spacing when the map (or Google Earth file) is made: the program works out the distance travelled from the clock and GPS track, and keeps the strongest reading (or the average) in each stretch. The recording is never changed.
- **Up to four radios, one per frequency slot.** Tick the radios you have in the new "Your radios" box; each of the four frequency slots has a Radio choice (Auto / Hermes / SDRplay). Example: the Hermes Lite 2 holds an AM-broadcast slot and two HF ham bands (all read at the same instant), while the SDRplay does airband and 70 cm. Auto places HF on the Hermes Lite 2 and everything else on the SDRplay. The design is open for a third radio type later.
- **Easier installing (Windows).** The installer now asks about your radio FIRST: a Hermes Lite 2-only install skips the SDRplay driver, PothosSDR and the exact-Python requirement completely. If you do use the SDRplay and PothosSDR is missing, the installer can now install it for you (through winget). One line at the top says you can press Enter at every question. The control panel starts with your radios already ticked.
- **Version 1.3.0** in the panel, guide, README and story page.
- **Hear and record Hermes slices - even while driving.** Each Hermes Lite 2 slice has Listen (one at a time), a mode choice (AM, SAM, USB, LSB, NFM) and Record. Several slices can record at once, each to its own WAV (12,000 samples/s, named with slice, frequency and start time) in the Recordings folder; recordings survive even if the program is stopped mid-file. Measurements are never disturbed - the sound is read from the same stream the radio is already sending.
- **Per-slice zoom slider.** Every slice has its own zoom slider (full width to x16), centred on the measured frequency, live while sliding, remembered per slice.
- **Slices in their own tab, full screen.** An Open-in-its-own-tab button shows only the four slices, large (2x2), with a Full screen button; sound, recording and zoom work there too.

## Big slot panels (update)
- Each of the four slots is a full-width panadapter with its own waterfall, stacked one under the other (Compact view still fits all four on one screen; Pop out opens one slot in its own tab). The separate wide panadapter box is gone: the slots replace it.
- Every slot has a **Radio** chooser: Off (skip this slot), Hermes or SDRplay. Any number of slots can be used; picking a radio switches a slot on.
- SDRplay slots have a **Live** button. The SDRplay has one tuner, so one SDRplay slot is live at a time (the lowest-numbered one until you choose) and gets every panadapter feature: centre and width of view, all modes (AM, SAM, USB, LSB, CW, FM, broadcast FM), sound, record audio, squelch, IF/RF attenuation and automatic gain. The other SDRplay slots keep their settings and show their last picture until you press their Live.
- Controls a radio does not have are hidden on that slot (for example the Hermes has no IF/RF attenuators; the SDRplay has no AGC choice).
- Every slot also has Lock, Jump to band, clickable known-frequency markers, a signal meter, a "what you hear" sound scope, shared memory frequencies and a grab-to-move dB scale.
- While logging, Hermes slots stay live; SDRplay slots show their readings until you pause.

## Fix: Start logging could fail with "No Hermes Lite 2 answered"
The live view could quietly reopen the Hermes Lite 2 just as logging was starting, and a streaming Hermes does not answer a second program. Now nothing can open the radios while the logger starts, the program waits a moment after closing the live view, and the logger retries finding the radio for several seconds.

## Fix: Start logging while the slots are live
The live view could reopen the Hermes Lite 2 in the instant logging was starting, so the logger found it busy ("No Hermes Lite 2 answered"). Nothing can open the radios while the logger starts now, the program gives the radio a moment to settle after closing the live view, and the logger retries finding the radio for several seconds.

The control panel now hands the Hermes' address to the logger, so Start logging no longer searches for the radio again. The Hermes slot panels stay on screen and carry on from the logger after a brief pause.
