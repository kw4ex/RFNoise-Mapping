# RFI Trail 2.0.0j (testing release)

Changes since 2.0.0i (tested on one Apple-silicon Mac; the Windows changes have not been tested on a Windows PC yet):

- **RTL-SDR dongles on the Mac.**
  - The installer has a new step 3b that installs the RTL-SDR drivers (soapysdr, soapyrtlsdr, librtlsdr) with Homebrew. Return means yes; answer n if you have no dongle.
  - The installer now builds RFI Trail's private Python with the same Python version as the radio library, which a dongle needs.
  - **Check my dongle** (Hardware setup, under RTL-SDR dongles) looks at the USB ports, the driver programs, rtl_test, SoapySDR, RFI Trail's own Python, programs that may be holding the dongle, and the "Your radios" ticks. It gives one plain answer and one-click fixes: **Use my dongle** (ticks RTL-SDR, unticks SDRplay when you have no other radio) and **Install the RTL-SDR drivers**.
  - **Install the RTL-SDR drivers** can be pressed at any time, also after RFI Trail is installed.
  - Each check is saved as a text report in the Reports folder, which the support file already collects.
- **FlexRadio.**
  - Flex slots can adopt a slice the radio already has, and tuning with the radio's dial is followed by the slot.
  - **Zoom** (− / slider / +) narrows or widens the radio's own panadapter, so a Flex zooms smoothly and shows real detail.
  - **Drag the yellow tuning bar** on the picture to retune; the bar follows the pointer while you drag. Clicking still tunes too.
  - **Centre** puts the tuned frequency in the middle of the picture. **Keep centred** (off by default) keeps it there while you tune; otherwise the picture stays still until you tune off its edge or press Centre.
  - **Mute radio speaker** silences the radio's own speaker, headphone and line-out jacks (the sound in the program is not affected).
  - The Flex is no longer let go while the page sits in a hidden tab: the page sends a small "still here" signal every 15 s, and the idle limit is longer.
- **GPS.**
  - The GPS wording is neutral: it works the same for GPS2IP, NMEA Gps and similar apps, and no longer names the wrong app.
  - The GPS box no longer contradicts itself (for example "No fix yet" with satellites counted); it says plainly what the app is sending, and a speed of -1 from iOS is shown as 0.
  - Typing 127.0.0.1 for the phone is handled; the Hardware setup text explains the hotspot address (172.20.10.1).
- **Radios and slots.**
  - Choosing a radio on a slot ticks it in **Your radios** (and the other way round); no more warning to fix by hand.
  - Radios whose driver is installed can be ticked even when they are not plugged in; the status line says "driver installed, radio not found" or "driver missing".
  - **Hide unused** (top bar and Settings) hides slots with no radio, so only the slots in use are shown.
- **Screen.**
  - Point at any button, box or picture and press **?** for its help; the top bar shows "Hover + ? = help".
  - Shorter top-bar labels so the line does not wrap (the full wording is in the hover text): Settings, Hardware, Hide unused, Quit, Guide.
  - Compact view: the slot header is at most two lines, the controls under the picture are two lines, the Highest meter box no longer jumps when the reading goes above S9, and the S-meter and level readouts no longer overlap other controls.
  - Car mode: the Highest meter box is tidy (dial with its name, big S reading, two short lines) and all boxes have the same height and show their text without cutting it off.
- Support checks that wrote extra files (a 10 s sound sample and tune-check lines from the Flex) are now off unless a support person turns them on.
- Same as 2.0.0i otherwise (see its notes for the later fixes that are included).
