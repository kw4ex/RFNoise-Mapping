Frequencies to measure (up to 4)
Low bands (0.75, 3.7, 7.3 MHz) show the general area. A VHF or AM airband (118–137 MHz) channel pinpoints a pole or house. Pick a quiet channel with no stations. You can change these while driving.
More settings



Leave gain alone during a drive so dots can be compared. If readings are pinned at the top or bottom, raise or lower the IF gain reduction.
