RFI TRAIL __VER__ for Mac  -  START HERE
==========================================

Find where radio noise (power lines, poles, noisy buildings) is strongest: drive a route with a radio and a GPS,
and the program draws the readings as coloured dots on a street map.

TO INSTALL (about 5 minutes, internet needed)
  1. If you have the zip, double-click it to unzip it. Keep the two folders  mac  and  app  side by side.
  2. Open the  mac  folder.
  3. RIGHT-CLICK  Install_RFI_Trail.command  and choose  Open,  then click  Open  again.
     (Only the first time: macOS asks because the file came from the internet.)
     If macOS says it "cannot be opened because Apple cannot check it": click Done (never Move to Trash), open
     System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway" and type your Mac password.
  4. In the window that opens, type 1 and press Return, then answer y (or just press Return) to each question.
     It explains every step before it does it.
  5. When it says DONE, a "RFI Trail" icon is on your Desktop and in Applications.

FIRST TRY (no radio needed)
  (A red PRACTICE MODE banner means the radios, GPS and sound are pretend. Press "Turn practice off" before a real drive.)
  Double-click the RFI Trail icon. In the page that opens press  Settings, then  "Try a practice drive + map".

ABOUT
  RFI Trail is the successor to RF Noise Map, designed and tested on the air by KW4EX.
  With thanks to Jeff Stuparits, W4DD, whose noise-mapping program and generous help inspired it.

NEED HELP?
  - In the program, point at anything and press the ? key (or F1) for a short explanation.
  - The "Help & guide" button at the top of the program opens the full guide. A printable copy is in this
    download: RFI_Trail_User_Guide.pdf (and RFI_Trail_Help.html).
  - mac/README_Mac.txt has the details (Hermes Lite 2 network setup, SDRplay driver, problems and fixes).
  - If the installer has a problem it explains the likely cause and can make one small "support file" (logs only,
    nothing is sent anywhere) to send to KW4EX.

UPDATING: run the newer version's installer the same way. Your drives, maps and settings (in the "RFI Trail"
folder in your home folder) are kept.
